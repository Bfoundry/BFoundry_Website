About Peter Bennett

Founder of BFoundry. More than 20 years leading transformation, from early-stage startups to global banks.

Where operations, technology and change meet

My career has been spent at the point where operations, technology and change come together, in startups and in some of the largest institutions in the world.

In startups the challenge is building the foundations while the business moves fast. In large institutions it’s changing something enormous while it keeps running. Both have taught me that good transformation is less about grand plans and more about clarity, sequencing and bringing people with you.

I reached Managing Director at Credit Suisse and UBS. Most recently I spent three years on the Group Technology and Operations integration management team bringing Credit Suisse into UBS, the first merger of two global systemically important banks. I led delivery assurance and chaired key governance forums, driving consistency, robust planning and delivery. As the plans matured, I also led the implementation of group-wide historical data and data archiving solutions, critical-path deliveries for the integration’s success. Doing that under intense regulatory and public scrutiny is a lesson in prioritisation, risk and leadership that I now bring to every engagement.

I founded BFoundry to put that experience to work for growing firms, working directly with the founders and teams doing the building.
