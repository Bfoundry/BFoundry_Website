Big-bank scale. Startup pace.

I’ve spent more than 20 years leading operations and technology change, from startups to two of the world’s largest banks, where I was a Managing Director at both Credit Suisse and UBS. Few environments are as complex or as tightly controlled, and the disciplines carry across to any sector.

Most recently I was part of the Group Technology and Operations integration management team bringing Credit Suisse into UBS: a three-year programme and the first merger of two global systemically important banks. I led delivery assurance and chaired key governance forums, driving consistency, robust planning and delivery. As the plans matured, I also led the implementation of group-wide historical data and data archiving solutions, critical-path deliveries for the integration’s success. It’s an extreme example, but the lessons apply at any size: be clear about the destination, sequence the work, control the risk and bring people with you.
