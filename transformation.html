Transformation consulting

Focused programmes that change how your business runs, with a clear scope, a clear outcome and senior, hands-on leadership from start to finish.

Where I can help

Programmes are shaped around the outcome you need. Most fall into one or more of these areas.

Digital process transformation

Map how work really gets done, remove manual steps and redesign processes around modern, connected digital tools.

Technology and AI adoption

Rationalise systems and vendors, set a pragmatic technology roadmap, and identify where AI and automation can safely add value.

Operating model and governance

Clarify who does what and how decisions get made, with oversight and reporting proportionate to your size and ambitions.

Integration and change delivery

Lead complex change, such as integrations, migrations and restructures, through to completion without losing control of risk.

What you can expect

Build · Lead · Grow, applied to a single programme.

Build

A short diagnostic to agree the problem, the target state, the plan and how we’ll measure success.

Lead

Senior, hands-on delivery. You work with me directly throughout, not a junior team learning on your time.

Grow

A clean handover, with documentation, governance and a team ready to own and extend what we’ve built.

Proven at the hardest end of the scale

The integration of Credit Suisse into UBS was the first merger of two global systemically important banks.

Over three years on the Group Technology and Operations integration management team, I led delivery assurance and chaired key governance forums, driving consistency, robust planning and delivery. As the plans matured, I also led the implementation of group-wide historical data and data archiving solutions, critical-path deliveries for the integration’s success. That meant changing enormous, interconnected businesses while they kept running, under intense regulatory and public scrutiny. Most programmes are far smaller, but the same disciplines decide whether they land.
