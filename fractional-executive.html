Fractional executive leadership

Experienced operating leadership for startups, scale-ups and established businesses, for part of the week, without the cost and commitment of a full-time hire.

When it makes sense

Most firms reach a point where the founders can’t carry operations, technology and governance as well as growth, but a permanent C-suite hire is premature or hard to justify.

  • You’re scaling quickly and the operating model, systems and processes haven’t kept up
  • Founders are spending too much time on operations instead of growth
  • You need senior leadership now but aren’t ready to hire permanently
  • A senior leader has left and the gap needs covering well
  • You need to modernise how the business works but have no one senior to lead it

A typical engagement

Role
Fractional COO, CTO or Chief Transformation Officer
Time
One to three days a week, flexing with what the business needs
Duration
Usually six to eighteen months
Way of working
On site and remote, as a member of your leadership team

What I take on

The scope is agreed with you up front and reviewed regularly. It usually covers some or all of these.

Operations

An operating model that scales: clear ownership, documented processes, sensible controls and the right partners.

Technology and AI

A pragmatic roadmap, well-managed vendors and safe, useful adoption of AI and automation where it genuinely adds value.

Governance

Risk, oversight and reporting proportionate to your stage, and strong enough for investors, auditors and major clients.

How it starts

A simple route from first conversation to a clear role and agreed outcomes.

Talk

A no-obligation conversation to understand your business, what’s in the way and whether I’m the right fit.

Diagnose

A short, fixed-scope review to agree the priorities and what success looks like at three, six and twelve months.

Engage

An agreed role, time commitment and set of outcomes, reviewed with you regularly and adjusted as you grow.
